Privacy Policy

Auto Clicker Pro — Tap, Hold & Swipe  ·  Effective: July 3, 2026

Short version: Auto Clicker Pro does not collect, store, or share any personal information. All your sequences and settings are saved locally on your device only.

1. Information We Collect

Auto Clicker Pro itself collects no personal information. The app stores your tap/hold/swipe sequences and settings exclusively in a local database on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

2. Accessibility Service

The app uses Android's Accessibility Service solely to dispatch touch gestures (taps, holds, and swipes) on your behalf. Specifically:

  • The service does not read, capture, or transmit any screen content (canRetrieveWindowContent is disabled).
  • It only listens for window-state events to know when to fire gestures you have configured.
  • No keystroke logging, clipboard access, or content scraping takes place.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to serve relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising identifier (Android Ad ID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device information (model, OS version)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can review Google's privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy, and opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google Settings.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's purchase and payment policies apply.

5. Permissions Explained

  • Draw over other apps — displays the floating control overlay while a sequence is running.
  • Accessibility Service — dispatches the touch gestures you configure on your behalf.
  • Post Notifications — shows a persistent status notification with an emergency "Stop now" action while a sequence is running (Android 13 and above).
  • Receive Boot Completed — optionally restores your last state after a device restart.
  • Internet / Network State — required by AdMob to load and display ads.

6. Data Storage & Security

All app data (sequences, preferences) is stored locally on your device. We have no servers and therefore no ability to access, modify, or delete your data remotely.

7. Children's Privacy

Auto Clicker Pro is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party SDK (e.g., AdMob), please contact Google directly to request its removal.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted here with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
