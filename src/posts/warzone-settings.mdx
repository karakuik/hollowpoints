Every season I end up tweaking something and forgetting what I changed. Writing it down this time.

## Video

- **FOV:** 105 — anything lower feels claustrophobic at this point
- **Render Resolution:** 100 (no reason to go lower on a 1440p monitor)
- **NVIDIA Reflex:** Enabled + Boost
- **V-Sync:** Off, always off

## Mouse

Running a **Logitech G Pro X Superlight 2** at 800 DPI. In-game sensitivity at 6.5, ADS multiplier at 1.0 (relative). I know some people love low ADS multiplier but I find it messes with my muscle memory when hipfiring.

## Controller (when I'm lazy)

Right stick max look rate 800, response curve Linear. Deadzone inner 0.05. I spent way too long on this.

## Audio

Headset: **SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro Wireless**. In-game audio mix set to **Boost High** — you'll hear footsteps from across the map. Slightly unrealistic, completely worth it.

## What I'm running

Currently grinding ranked with a **RAM-7 + Renetti** loadout. The RAM is genuinely busted this season. Don't tell anyone.
