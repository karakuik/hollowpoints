I've played through Pokémon Crystal probably eight times. Maybe nine. I stopped counting around 2019.

## The obvious stuff

Johto's level design is genuinely good in a way the later games never quite matched. Two regions, a villain team that actually has a coherent motive, a rival who feels like a real antagonist instead of a cheerful inconvenience.

The Gen 2 Pokémon designs are also just... better? Heracross, Tyranitar, Espeon, Umbreon. There's a restraint to them that later generations lost. Not every one is a hit — Dunsparce exists — but the hits are really good.

## The real reason

It's the scale. Crystal is small enough that you can hold it in your head. I know where everything is. I know which routes are worth grinding, which ones to skip, which shops have what.

Modern Pokémon games are big and full of things to do. Crystal is compact and full of things to *find*. That's a different feeling entirely.

## The Pokédex thing

I built the viability guide on this site partly as a reference for my own runs. If you're doing a solo challenge or trying to figure out whether Ampharos is worth evolving into (it is, by the way — UU tier, solid SpA) — check the [Crystal Pokédex](/pokedex).

## One more thing

The shiny animation in Crystal is still the best shiny reveal in any Pokémon game. The sparkle on a black and white GBC screen hits different.

That's it. That's the whole argument.
